# SIH Live Demo Storyboard

**Total Time:** 2 Minutes

### Scene 1: The Pristine Dashboard (0:00 - 0:30)
* **Action:** Open the browser to `http://localhost:3000`. Show the clean, light-mode Landing Page. Click "Launch Console" to log in.
* **Talking Point:** "Welcome to the Revamp AI Enterprise console. As an intelligence operator, I am greeted by a modern, glassmorphic dashboard tracking live telemetry of all artifacts we've generated today."
* **Visual:** Point out the Threat Severity bar charts and the global scrolling threat ticker at the bottom of the screen.

### Scene 2: The Ingestion Wizard (0:30 - 1:00)
* **Action:** Click "Initialize Target" to open the `NewTransformationPage`.
* **Talking Point:** "A critical zero-day vulnerability just hit our network. I don't have time to write 4 different reports."
* **Action:** Click the "Smart Fill" button (this populates the textarea with the CVE-2026-9912 Active Directory threat).
* **Talking Point:** "I paste the raw, chaotic threat log here. I select my target audience as 'Executive' and urgency as 'Critical'."

### Scene 3: The Agent Boot Sequence (1:00 - 1:30)
* **Action:** Click "Execute Pipeline". 
* **Visual:** The screen transitions to the Job Processing loader.
* **Talking Point:** "As soon as I execute, our backend FastAPI server takes over. You can see the live LangGraph Orchestrator spinning up. It is actively calling the Sarvam AI API, spawning parallel agents to write the LinkedIn posts, the Technical Advisory, and the CISO Briefing simultaneously."

### Scene 4: The Final Workspace (1:30 - 2:00)
* **Action:** The loader finishes and redirects to the `TransformationResultPage`.
* **Visual:** Click through the tabs on the left (CISO Briefing, Technical Advisory, Social Campaign).
* **Talking Point:** "In under 5 seconds, the chaos is turned into clarity. Here is our CISO Briefing, structured perfectly. If I click 'Technical Advisory', we have the exact extracted IoCs ready for the SOC team. And under 'Social Campaign', we have a LinkedIn post optimized for exactly 450 characters. Zero manual effort, total operational efficiency."

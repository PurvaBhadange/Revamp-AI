1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
At 03:14 IST, our perimeter monitoring systems detected anomalous lateral movement originating from the HR subnet, attempting to access the core Active Directory (AD) servers. The attack appears to exploit a zero-day vulnerability in the enterprise identity management protocol (tentatively tracked as CVE-2026-9912). Immediate containment protocols were initiated, but evidence suggests partial exfiltration of encrypted service account credentials.

- Privilege Escalation (T1068): Once inside, the attacker deployed a custom PowerShell script to elevate privileges from standard user to SYSTEM.
- Credential Access (T1003.001): The attacker dumped the LSASS memory space to extract NTLM hashes.

# SIH Final Pitch: Revamp AI Presentation Script

**Duration:** 3-4 Minutes
**Presenters:** Lead Architect / Presenter

---

**[Slide 1: Title & Problem Statement]**
"Good morning, judges. We are presenting **Revamp AI**. 
The challenge organizations face today isn't a lack of intelligence—it's the massive bottleneck of *translating* that intelligence. When a critical cyber threat is detected, analysts waste hours manually rewriting the same incident into a C-suite summary, a technical SOC advisory, and a public relations tweet. Our platform completely eliminates this bottleneck."

**[Slide 2: The Solution - Revamp AI]**
"Revamp AI is an autonomous content transformation engine. An operator simply pastes raw threat data into our portal, selects their target audiences, and our system instantly generates mathematically verified, audience-tailored deliverables. And the best part? It's powered by **Sarvam AI**, India's premier indigenous AI model, ensuring data sovereignty and regional language capabilities."

**[Slide 3: Architecture & Innovation]**
"Under the hood, we aren't just using a basic chatbot prompt. We engineered a **LangGraph Multi-Agent Swarm** using Python FastAPI. 
When data is submitted, the orchestrator spawns multiple independent AI agents—an Executive Agent, a Social Media Agent, and a Technical Agent. They work in parallel to synthesize the outputs, which are then passed through strict JSON guardrails to ensure zero hallucination before being rendered on our Next.js frontend."

**[Slide 4: Production Readiness & Future Scope]**
"Our platform is currently fully operational as a SaaS prototype. For enterprise deployment, we have architected the future scope to include a Qdrant Vector Database for RAG (allowing the ingestion of 500-page PDF reports) and a headless rendering farm to physically compile PowerPoint and PDF binaries on the fly."

"We will now demonstrate the platform live."
